## The Problem We Solve

Small nonprofits and mission-driven organizations lose 8–20 hours per week to tasks that don't require human judgment: searching grant databases, formatting LOIs, researching partner organizations, drafting impact summaries. Staff burn out. Opportunities get missed. The work never catches up.

You don't need a consultant. You need an observable agent that does the research, shows its work, and hands you the output.

---

## What You Get: The 30-Day Growth Mission

A single named Yappyverse agent — Grant Scout 🔍 — works your specific growth bottleneck for 30 days. Every action is logged to your public (or private) Impact Ledger. You see exactly what was done, when, and why.

### Scope (what's authorized)

- Grant opportunity research: federal, state, foundation, and corporate sources  
- Eligibility screening against your mission, budget size, and geography  
- Grant summary reports: deadline, funder priorities, typical award size, fit score  
- Draft LOI outlines (structure + key claims — you write the final copy)  
- Partner organization identification: who funds similar work, who's looking for co-applicants  
- Weekly impact digest: what was found, what was skipped, what's next

### Out of scope (what the agent will NOT do)

- Sign or submit any application on your behalf  
- Make financial commitments  
- Contact funders without your explicit approval  
- Handle donor data, health data, or legal documents  
- Operate outside the authorized mission scope defined at contract signing
