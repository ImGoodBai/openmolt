## Discovery Conversation Script (3 questions)

These three questions work for any warm call. Listen for specificity — vague answers mean the pain isn't felt yet.

**Q1: Diagnose the problem**  
"How much time does your team spend each month on grant research — searching, screening, checking eligibility, summarizing?"

*Listen for:* Hours per week, staff frustration, missed deadlines, applying to things they didn't qualify for.

**Q2: Quantify the cost**  
"Have you missed a grant deadline because you didn't have bandwidth to prep, or applied for something you later realized you weren't eligible for?"

*Listen for:* Specific missed opportunities, wasted application hours, staff burnout.

**Q3: Test the offer**  
"If someone could hand you a verified map of 15+ grants you're actually eligible to pursue — every one confirmed from the primary source, with eligibility evidence and a deadline calendar — would that be useful?"

*If yes:* "We've built that service. 30 days, $2,500, full refund if we don't deliver. Can I send you what the deliverable looks like?"

## Disqualifiers — Walk Away If

- They want a free pilot "to evaluate" — offer a discounted paid pilot at $1,500 if needed, never free
- Decision requires board vote or procurement committee — not a founding pilot candidate
- They want to "check back in a few months" — no urgency, no pain, move to backup list
- They want the agent to write and submit applications on day one — scope mismatch, not ready

## What Not to Claim During Outreach

Do not represent Grant Scout as:
- Fully autonomous
- Production-proven at scale
- Part of a verified reputation network
- Using live economic Beads

**What to say:**  
"Founding pilot of an AI-assisted, human-supervised grant intelligence service. Every action is reviewed before delivery. We're building the track record — you're one of the first three clients."
